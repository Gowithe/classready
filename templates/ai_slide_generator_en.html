FAQ

Can I use this for any subject?

Yes. Clazzee is built for teachers across subjects. Start generic now, then create subject-specific templates later.

Is text-to-speech supported?

Yes on modern browsers (Chrome/Edge). If voices load slowly, Clazzee waits and retries automatically.

Can I add images from my computer?

Yes. Upload images while editing slides, and they’ll appear in the viewer.

Does Clazzee replace PowerPoint?

Think of Clazzee as a teaching-first slide system: interactive reveal + in-class tools + classroom pacing.
